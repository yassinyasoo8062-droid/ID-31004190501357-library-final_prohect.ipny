# Data Integrity Report

The data was checked for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent values, and orphan records.

* Missing checkout IDs were filled with new unique IDs.
* Missing member information was restored from the original members table when available.
* Membership status and neighborhood names were standardized.
* Missing grades were filled using the median grade.
* Join dates and return dates were converted to datetime format.
* Missing publication years were restored from the original book catalog when available.
* No duplicate records were found.
* No orphan book records were found.
* Five orphan member records were identified and preserved.
